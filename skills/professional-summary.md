# Professional summary

2–4 lines (40–70 words). It answers: who are you professionally, what is your strongest evidence, and why this role?

**Pattern:** [Title/identity matching the target role] with [N years / domain] in [specialisms the job wants]. [One or two signature achievements with numbers]. [What you bring to *this* employer/role — optional].

- Write in implied first person (no "I", no "he/she"), present tense.
- Use the target role's language; no clichés ("dynamic", "passionate", "results-driven").
- Every claim must be supported later in the CV and by the profile.

Example (illustrative): "Data engineer with 6 years building cloud data platforms in fintech. Led the migration of 120 pipelines to Snowflake and dbt, cutting reporting latency from 24h to 15 min. Brings strong data-modelling and stakeholder skills to analytics-heavy product teams."
