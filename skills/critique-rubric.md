# Critique rubric

Score each dimension 0–10. Be calibrated and strict: 5 is average, 7 is good, 9+ is exceptional and rare.

| Dimension | 9–10 | 6–8 | 3–5 | 0–2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Relevance** | Every must-have visibly evidenced; headline/summary mirror the role | Most must-haves covered; some generic content | Several must-haves missing or buried | Not targeted at this job |
| **Impact** | Most bullets show quantified or concrete outcomes | Mix of outcomes and duties | Mostly duties | Lists of responsibilities only |
| **Clarity** | Tight, specific, scannable; no clichés | Mostly clear, some wordy bullets | Vague, long or jargon-heavy | Hard to follow |
| **Structure** | Logical order, consistent formatting, right length, ATS-friendly | Minor inconsistencies | Inconsistent or wrong length | Disorganised |
| **Accuracy** | Every claim traceable to the profile | Minor unsupported wording | An unsupported metric/skill | Invented roles, dates, metrics or qualifications |

## Procedure
1. List the job's key requirements (must-haves first) and mark each met / partial / missing with the CV evidence.
2. Read every line for outright mistakes: spelling, grammar, tense, inconsistent dates/punctuation, wrong company/job names, broken structure, placeholders. Give the exact fix.
3. Cross-check every fact (employers, titles, dates, numbers, qualifications, skills, contact details) against the profile. Anything not in the profile is "unsupported"; anything conflicting is "contradicts_profile"; inflated scope/seniority is "exaggerated".
4. Consider the automated check results you are given; confirm or dismiss them in your mistakes/factual issues.
5. Give prioritised, concrete improvements (what to change and how), and questions to ask the candidate where missing information would raise the score.

Do not reward length or keyword density for its own sake. Do not suggest inventing information.
