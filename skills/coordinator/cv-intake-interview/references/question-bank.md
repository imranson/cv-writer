# Question bank

Use 1–3 of these per turn, adapted to the user's words.

## Software / data engineering
- Which systems did you design or own end to end? What scale (requests/s, users, data volume)?
- What did you improve: latency, cost, reliability (incidents, uptime), delivery speed?
- Which languages, frameworks and cloud services did you use in production?

## Sales / business development
- What was your quota and attainment (% of target) each year?
- Biggest deal or account won — value and how you won it?
- Did you open new markets, segments or partnerships?

## Design / product
- Which products or features did you ship, and what did they change (conversion, retention, NPS)?
- How did you research and validate decisions? Who did you collaborate with?
- Links to a portfolio or case studies?

## Operations / project management
- Budgets, headcount or vendors you managed?
- Processes you introduced or fixed — what time or money did they save?
- Projects delivered on time / under budget — sizes and outcomes?

## Graduates and early careers
- Dissertation or capstone topic and result? Relevant modules?
- Internships, part-time work, societies, hackathons, volunteering — what did you achieve?
- Tools and skills you've used hands-on, even outside paid work?

## Career changers
- Which achievements from your previous field transfer (leadership, analysis, customer work)?
- What have you done to build skills for the new field (courses, projects, certifications)?
- Why this change — one sentence we could use in the summary?
