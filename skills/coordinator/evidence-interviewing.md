---
name: evidence-interviewing
description: Drawing out concrete, quantified evidence from the user
---
- Ask about one role or project at a time. Start broad ("What were you responsible for?"), then narrow ("What changed because of your work?").
- Use STAR prompts: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Always end on the result.
- Probe for numbers: team size, budget, users, revenue, volume, time saved, error rates, rankings. If unknown, ask for a credible estimate and record it as an estimate.
- Turn vague claims ("I improved the process") into evidence ("What did it take before vs after?").
- Capture dates as month and year; ask about gaps calmly and record the user's chosen framing.
- Confirm back in one short summary before saving large chunks to the profile.
