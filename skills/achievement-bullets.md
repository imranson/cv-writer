# Writing achievement bullets

A bullet should prove capability with evidence, not describe duties.

**Formula:** Strong action verb + what you did (scope, tools, stakeholders) + measurable result or business outcome.
Order can flex ("Cut churn 12% by …") — lead with whatever is most impressive.

## Rules
- Start with a past-tense action verb for past roles, present tense for the current role. Never start with "Responsible for", "Helped", "Worked on", "Duties included".
- One idea per bullet; 1–2 lines (roughly 15–30 words). Cut filler ("successfully", "various", "in order to").
- Quantify wherever the profile supports it: money, %, time saved, volume, users, team size, frequency, ranking. If no number exists, show scope (who/how many/how often) or a concrete before→after.
- **Never invent numbers.** If a metric would help but is not in the profile, leave it out and flag it as a question for the candidate.
- Name the tools/methods that matter for the target job, in context ("Built a dbt + Airflow pipeline…") rather than in a disconnected list.
- No first person (I, my, we). No full stops mixed with no full stops — be consistent.
- 3–6 bullets for recent/relevant roles; 1–3 for older or less relevant ones. Order bullets by relevance to the target job.

## Strong verbs by theme
- Lead: directed, led, mentored, coached, mobilised, chaired
- Build: built, designed, engineered, launched, implemented, shipped, automated
- Improve: cut, reduced, accelerated, streamlined, consolidated, raised, doubled
- Win/grow: secured, negotiated, won, grew, expanded, converted
- Analyse: diagnosed, modelled, forecast, quantified, audited

## Rewrites
- Weak: "Responsible for managing the company's social media accounts."
  Strong: "Grew Instagram following from 4k to 19k in 10 months by launching a weekly creator series and A/B-testing post formats."
- Weak: "Worked on improving the checkout process."
  Strong: "Redesigned the checkout flow with product and UX, cutting cart abandonment from 71% to 58% and adding £1.1m annualised revenue."
- Weak: "Helped new starters."
  Strong: "Mentored 6 junior analysts through onboarding; 4 were promoted within 18 months."

(All numbers above are illustrative — only use figures that appear in the candidate's profile.)
