---
name: achievement-bullets
description: Writing strong, truthful CV bullets. Load before writing or editing experience bullets
---
- Pattern: strong past-tense verb + what you did + how + measurable result. Present tense only for the current role.
- Lead each role with the bullets most relevant to the target job; 3–6 bullets per recent role, fewer for older ones.
- One idea per bullet, ideally one line, at most two.
- Prefer specifics over adjectives: "cut release time from 2 days to 3 hours" beats "significantly improved delivery".
- Avoid: "responsible for", "helped with", "various", "team player", "hard-working", first-person pronouns.
- Use the job's vocabulary where it truthfully describes the user's work; never add a skill the profile does not support.
