---
name: review-rubric
description: Scoring rubric for CV critiques
---
Score out of 10 by weighting:
- 3 pts Relevance: top third of page 1 makes the case for this job; key requirements are evidenced.
- 3 pts Evidence: specific, quantified achievements rather than duties.
- 2 pts Clarity: scannable structure, consistent dates and formatting, no filler, sensible length.
- 1 pt ATS: standard headings, real text (no keyword lists without evidence), job keywords present.
- 1 pt Fit to preferences: tone, length, spelling and section order match the CV's preferences.
Deduct for any claim that looks fabricated or inconsistent and say so first.
