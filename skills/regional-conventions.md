# Regional conventions

| | UK / Ireland | US / Canada | Continental Europe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | CV | Resume (CV = academic) | CV (Europass sometimes) |
| Length | 2 pages typical | 1 page (≤10 yrs), 2 max | 1–2 pages |
| Spelling | British (optimise, organisation, programme) | American (optimize, organization, program) | Usually British English for English CVs |
| Photo / DOB | No | No (discrimination law) | Common in DE/FR/IT etc. — ask |
| Personal statement | "Profile" | "Summary" | "Profile" |
| References | Omit | Omit | Omit |

- Follow the spelling preference in the candidate profile; otherwise match the job listing's locale.
- Grades: UK "First-class honours / 2:1", US "GPA 3.8/4.0" — keep the candidate's original system.
- Use the job location's date conventions consistently (Jan 2024 – Mar 2025 is universally safe).
