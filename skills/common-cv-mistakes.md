# Common CV mistakes

**Outright errors (fix always)**
- Typos, wrong capitalisation of technologies (Javascript → JavaScript, Github → GitHub, Powerpoint → PowerPoint).
- Inconsistent date formats, tense errors (past roles in present tense), mixed punctuation at bullet ends.
- Overlapping or impossible dates; dates that disagree with the profile.
- Placeholder text, template leftovers, wrong company name from a previous application.
- Broken contact details.

**Content weaknesses**
- Duty lists instead of achievements; "responsible for".
- Clichés with no proof: "hard-working team player", "results-driven", "passionate", "go-getter", "detail-oriented".
- Generic summary that could belong to anyone; objective statements about what the candidate wants.
- Burying the most relevant experience; equal space for irrelevant old roles.
- Skills lists with everything ever touched, or soft skills listed without evidence.
- Unexplained acronyms and internal jargon.
- Exaggeration: "led" when they contributed, team-level results claimed personally.

**Presentation**
- Too long for the candidate's level; dense walls of text; tiny fonts or cramped margins.
- Photos, age, marital status, "References available on request" (UK/US norms).
